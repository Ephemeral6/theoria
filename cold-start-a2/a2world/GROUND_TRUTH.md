## Rules

| name | when | then |
|---|---|---|
| `push` | act=D and the target cell is floor (or the opened Door cell) | Cart moves one cell in direction D |
| `blocked` | act=D and the target cell is a wall, the closed Door, or the Button | nothing moves |
| `press` | act=UP and the Cart is at (2,1), so the target is the Button, and the Button is unpressed | Button recolours 7 -> 8 AND the Door vanishes, in the same transition; the Cart does not move |
| `teleport` | act=DOWN and the target cell is the Portal entry (7,4) — which is only ever true from the Door cell (6,4), and only after the Door has opened | Cart moves to (7,6), inside the right room |

## Invariants

* **cart_unique** — exactly one cell carries colour 6 at all times
* **button_latched** — once the Button is 8 it is never 7 again
* **pocket_unreachable** — (7,1) is floor walled on all four sides; no state with the Cart there is reachable, with or without the teleport. TRUE of the world — this is what the repaired manual proves.
* **right_room_locked_WITHOUT_teleport** — delete the teleport and the right room is unreachable, because column c5 is solid wall from r1 to r7. FALSE of the world — this is the exhibit.
