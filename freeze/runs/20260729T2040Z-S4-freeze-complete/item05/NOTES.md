## 6. Caveat (b) — `lp_potential` sound but incomplete. Verdict: true as stated, and materially incomplete as a caveat

* "never certifies a solvable configuration" — asserted by design and by test,
  **D-014**, `DECISIONS.md:242-255`; README:57-62 "the method may fail to prove a
  true statement, but it can never prove a false one".
* "some genuinely unsolvable ones admit no linear pagoda" — **true**, the
  witness is Fixture C `0111` (D-014). Scale from E11: **639/2189 = 29.2 %** of
  genuinely unreachable worlds at N=3000 (`ENGINE_TABLE.md` row 4).
* **What CLAUDE.md does not say, and a freeze manifest must.** Soundness holds
  *over the move list the engine is handed*. E17
  (`runs/20260729T034043Z-E17-held-out-validation`, reported in `ENGINE_TABLE.md`
  row 4) withheld one jump geometry at a time and found **58 certificates
  outright false against BFS ground truth**, with a hand-checkable witness on
  `peg4` (goal `0100`, start `0011`, geometry `jump(3,2,1)` withheld) — a
  certificate for a goal the fixture's own table says is *reachable*. The emit
  gate `premises_against_graph` does not catch it: handed the graph a
  partial-evidence caller actually holds, **all 1408 are emitted, the 58 false
  ones included, each carrying `holds: true` and `sound_over_graph: true`** into
  the shared candidate stream.
  So "sound" is true under the declared quantifier and **false under truncated
  evidence**, and the arm is a truncated-evidence caller by construction. Second
  qualifier, from `potential.py:282-289`: `no_linear_pagoda` is infeasibility
  *inside* the hard-coded `bound=10` weight box — E11 found 1 world in 3000 whose
  pagoda lives outside it. Both belong in the manifest; neither is in CLAUDE.md.
