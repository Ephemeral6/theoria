## 1 · The one-paragraph answer

On A0 the ablated arm is **indistinguishable from the full arm on everything a
benchmark scores** — same verdict, same plan, same plan length, replay equal to
the pixel, same behavioural and held-out accuracy — while costing strictly less
in every unit that could be measured. On A2 it is handed an unreachability
theorem that type-checks and is **false of the world**, and it believes it: it
settles the UNSAT bare, its surprise bus stays empty, its loop does not turn, and
a level solvable in 18 moves is archived as impossible. The full arm, on the same
manual and the same evidence, proves the same false theorem — and is then refuted
by a witness, localises the fault, probes, repairs, and re-plans to SAT/18.

**The cut buys a real saving and pays for it with the ability to tell a true
theorem from a false one.** That is the sentence this arm exists to license.

## 3 · Where a comparable number could not be got — every place

The work order asked for this to be said out loud rather than left blank, so all
five are here, including the two that are uncomfortable.

**(a) Theorize rounds. Not comparable, and this is the important one.**
This arm **never theorized**. `build_theory.py` builds its manuals by
mechanically downgrading the full arm's DSL — `cold-start-a0/theory/theory.dsl` →
`theory/a0_base.dsl`, `[status: proven]` → `[status: empirical]`, theorem clauses
deleted — with both sha256s recorded per file in
[`theory/DOWNGRADE_REPORT.json`](theory/DOWNGRADE_REPORT.json). That is A4a's
design choice and it is the right one: a re-theorized manual would be a *second*
difference between the arms, and `Theoria.md:280` says a second difference makes
the first unattributable. But it means there is no ablated-arm theorize count to
put beside the full arm's.

The full arm's own figure is weak from the other side too:
`cold-start-a0/A0_REPORT.md` §6.1 records that on A0 *"the theorize→certify inner
loop never ran… The revision count is 0, and a revision count of 0 is not a
success metric."* So even `1 pass, 0 revisions` measures a cold start, not a
loop. **On A2 the full arm's loop does run** — beats M0, M5, L1…L5, all recorded
`pass` — and that is where this row appears in earnest (§4).

**(b) The consequence for §2 that must not be blurred.** Because the two arms
hold the same manual, the equal replay and equal score are **partly by
construction**. What they establish is that the incision did not damage the
representation layer — which is precisely what P-1 and P-2 were pre-registered to
test — and **not** that the ablated arm *induces* as good a manual. Anyone
quoting the 0.987288 as "the ablated arm learns as well" is misquoting it.
(`calibration.json:predictions[P-2].caveat`, pinned by a test.)

**(c) Dollars.** Neither arm spent any on A0 or A2. Both cold starts are offline
replays, `proxy/var/` holds no ledger for either, and this arm is offline by
construction (`ledger_abl.py:9`). Reporting `$0 vs $0` would be true and carry no
information, so dollars is **not measured** rather than a tie. The full arm's
only metered spending is in `theoria-arm/runs/` on live ARC games (g50t), which
is a different world with no ablated counterpart. That is a real gap in the
paper's cost story and it is not closable offline.

**(d) Surprises on the bus, full-arm column.** `ablcore/surprise.py` is *this
arm's* construction (`DESIGN.md` §7.3), built so both arms could share one
scheduling rule; only one arm has actually been instrumented with it. The full
arm's cell is **not measured**, not 0.

**(e) E3 is not constructible on this material** — pre-registered by `DESIGN.md`
§10 item 3 and reported as a falsifier by A4a, not by me. `pddl_addressable` is
now dead code: the PDDL generator grounds the Portal entry cell with the
workaround off, so the complete manual plans SAT either way and the designed
construction cannot start. What E3 defended survives, measured elsewhere — see
§4's charity control. What is genuinely lost is a clean demonstration that a
planner's UNSAT can be a fact about the *encoding* rather than about the world.

## 4 · A2 — the expected failure, exhibited

### The theorem

`cold-start-a2/artifacts/exhibit_report.json` carries
`"exhibit_is_false_of_the_world": true`. The holed manual is missing the teleport
rule; from it the full arm derives `right_room_locked` — *no reachable state
satisfies Goal* — and Lean signs it with `#print axioms` returning `[]`, i.e.
**proved from the manual alone, no `sorry`, no axiom**. The world is solvable in
18 moves. The theorem is machine-checked and false.

Its falseness is a property of the **manual**, not of the prover, and upstream
states the constructive ground before anything runs: column c5 is solid wall from
r1 to r7, every rule left in the manual moves the Cart one adjacent cell, so
(2,7) is unreachable *under the manual*.

That last one is the sharpest form of the finding. E1's world is *really*

same for both**: verdict, settled, settled_by, certificate_owed,
directed_probes_scheduled, distinguishes_proof_from_exhaustion,
cheap_layer_green, surprises, loop_turns, theorize_owed. The full arm separates
them without difficulty: on E1 the theorem survives its probes and is archived
proved; on E2 it is refuted, localised, probed, repaired, re-planned.

Nothing here is a bug. The fields are identical **because the cut removed the
only machinery whose output would have differed.**

### The reviewer's punch, answered inside the exhibit

*"你没给它反例，当然它修不好"* — you never handed it a counterexample, of course
it did not repair. The mother-tree recon answered this against the design's prior
argument, and A4a recorded the correction rather than hiding it: `locate.py`
survives the ablation byte for byte, because it needs only a compiled manual and
one real solution path and reads no `.lean`.

So `exhibits/e2_a2.charity_control` hands the holed manual the world's solved
episode for free — and the ablated arm localises **correctly**:
`culprits = ['mispredicted_step']`, exactly one disagreeing step, the same answer
the full arm's `locate_report.json` gives.

**The ablation did not remove the ability to repair. It removed the thing that
goes and gets the counterexample.** The repair machinery is intact and idle, and
it is idle for a reason derived from the incision: no obligation → no theorem →
no `depends:` clause → no directed probe → nothing on the bus → the gate never
opens. `run_arm.py` contains the repair beats in its beat list and reaches them
`if bus.turns_the_loop()`; the loop not turning is a consequence, not a deletion.

### And the sweep neither arm had

`raw_trace.jsonl` — the fuller 248-frame record — goes red at t=184, cell (6,4),

both**, because a surprise the arm could not have had is not a surprise; putting

It is reported as the size of the gap between the two readings — green on the
evidence its theorizer had, red on the sweep — which is `Theoria.md` §1.3's claim
and exactly its limit.

## 5 · Engineering thrift and understanding thrift — the distinction, precisely

`Theoria.md:274` charges this arm with cutting the two apart, and `Theoria.md:376`
calls it 活命臂 because the review's first punch is *"你只是发了 diff"*. Here is
the cut, in the form the measurements license:

> **Engineering thrift** is every saving that survives when the proof obligation
> is deleted. On A0 it is the whole of the arm's performance: object-level
> representation, DSL, replay, planner, six engines, the four cheap-layer
> checks — all present, all byte-identical to the full arm, all reachable by
> anyone who read the paper's representation half and nothing else. It is real,
> it is large, and **anyone can copy it.** Measured: the ablated arm matches the
> full arm on 14 of 14 comparable A0 quantities while burning 0 seconds of
> proof, emitting 0 bytes of formalisation, spending 0 world steps on probes and
> executing 0 repair beats.
>
> **Understanding thrift** is the saving that only the proof obligation can buy,
> and it is invisible in every one of those numbers. It is the capacity to be
> **wrong out loud** — to state a universal claim in a form a witness can
> contradict, and therefore to be forced into the repair that a bare search
> result never triggers. Measured: on A2 the full arm and the ablated arm
> produce the *same* UNSAT from the *same* manual on the *same* evidence. The
> full arm converts it into a named, machine-checked theorem, is refuted by a
> single 18-step episode, and ends with a manual that solves the level. The
> ablated arm converts it into an archive entry and ends with a solvable level
> filed as impossible — and **10 of 10 of its own decision fields are identical
> to the ones it records on a level that really is impossible.**

The one-line form: **the ablated arm is cheaper, simpler, and just as accurate —
and it cannot tell a true theorem from a false one.** Its judgements about
impossibility are the same sentence whether they are true or false, because it
has deleted the only apparatus that would have made the difference show up.

Two clarifications the measurements force, both against the intuitive reading:

1. **The proof did not prevent the false claim.** The full arm proved
   `right_room_locked` too, axiom-free, and it was false. What proof bought was
   not correctness but **refutability** — a claim precise enough that a witness
   could kill it, and an obligation strong enough to go and look for the witness.
   A framework that never states the claim is not thereby safer; it is merely
   unfalsifiable, which is worse.
2. **The gain is not "it can repair".** Handed a counterexample, the ablated arm
   repairs perfectly well. The gain is that something *schedules the experiment*.
   `Theoria.md:43`'s three-way — mispredicted step / wrong goal test / misread
   board — is only exhaustive because a *proof* stands behind the impossibility;
   a planner's UNSAT could always be a fact about the encoding instead. This arm
   settles on `search`, so it has no ground on which the three-way is exhaustive
   at all.

## 7 · Limits, printed with the conclusion as §10 item 5 requires

1. **Two self-built offline worlds. Zero API contact. Zero sealed-pile contact.**
   This calibrates a **mechanism**, not an effect size on ARC. No number here
   transfers to a live game as a magnitude.
2. **The two arms share a manual by design.** Score and replay equality is
   partly constructive and says nothing about induction (§3(b)).
3. **Neither arm's A0 run exercised the inner loop**, so "theorize rounds" has no
   comparable pair on A0. The A2 fork is where the full arm's loop actually turns.
4. **Lean seconds are wall clock** and differ run to run. The load-bearing half
   of that row is the constructive zero on the ablated side.
5. **The dollar column is empty in both cells** and cannot be filled offline.
   Closing it needs the full arm and an ablated arm on the same live game, which
   this arm has no harness for (`STATUS.md`: no env loop, no model desk, no HTTP).
