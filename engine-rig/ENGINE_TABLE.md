# engine-rig · the eight processes, and what each one is worth

Generated by `python -m tools.engine_table`. **Do not edit by hand** — every
number here is probed out of an artifact under `runs/`, and the generator exits
non-zero if any of them has drifted. The provenance table at the end names the
file and the field each number came from.

One row per process: what it solves, where it was validated, how the claim was
re-checked by something that did not produce it, and its **known boundary**.

**The boundary column is never blank.** Where a boundary has not been measured
the cell says **边界未测** and states what measuring it would take. A table
with every cell confidently filled would suggest every cell had been tested, and
for one of these eight processes that is not true.

## How to read the boundary column

Three things are kept apart, because collapsing them is how a table like this
lies:

* **Measured boundary** — somebody drew the line and reports where it is.
* **Pre-registered boundary** — the line was written down in `DECISIONS.md`
  before it was hit, and a later measurement quantified it. `zero_space`'s
  quantifier (D-003) and `lp_potential`'s incompleteness (D-014) are these; an
  adversarial review overturned the word "defect" on the first of them.
* **边界未测** — nobody has drawn the line. `ic3_pdr` entire; the fuzz
  battery's view of a real Fast Downward rung; `lp_potential`'s infeasibility
  claim as a proof rather than a solver's word; `cegis_miner` past 3 literals
  and past the grid family; `probe_frontier`'s planner-backed path.

Two numbers are corrections of figures that were circulating, and both run
against the rig's own interest:

* `lp_potential`'s incompleteness is **639 / 2189 = 29.2 %**, not the 46.6 %
  often quoted — that figure is the no-certificate rate, and 24.0 pp of it is the
  engine correctly refusing to prove something false.
* `deadlock_carver`'s pruning dividend is real against a blind search and **zero**
  against an admissible heuristic (47 -> 47 on `lmcut`). The frequency half of
  Theoria 1.9 stands; the speed-up half does not.

## What audits all eight, and what it does not reach

* The property battery runs 500 worlds per engine with 0 violations, and
  55 injected defects establish that every invariant has a working detection
  path — 15 of them survive, which is the honest half of that sentence.
* It covers **six** of the eight. `deadlock_carver` and `ic3_pdr` have no property
  module. `deadlock_carver` is nonetheless the most independently adjudicated
  process here — three separate routes, one of them Lean — and `ic3_pdr` is the
  least.
* Of the **111** leaf fields the six engines publish into `candidates.jsonl`,
  **64** are asserted by no invariant. These reach the manual, and from there
  the adjudicating LLM's beliefs, carrying no evidence at all.
* The certificate rechecker catalogues 31 ways to lie to it and refuses all but
  2; the one that works is `delete-the-rule` — a certificate true of a rule set
  with a rule missing — which is Theoria §1.3 entire and which no certificate
  checker can see.
