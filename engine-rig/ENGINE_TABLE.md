Generated by `python -m tools.engine_table`. **Do not edit by hand** — every

(`artifacts/candidates.jsonl`, `fuzzlab/out/`) -- and the generator exits
non-zero if any of them has drifted. The provenance table at the end names the
file and the field each number came from.

One row per process: what it solves, where it was validated, how the claim was

the cell says **边界未测** and states what measuring it would take. A table
with every cell confidently filled would suggest every cell had been tested, and
for one of these eight processes that is not true.

## The three kinds of re-check, and which rows have which

* **证书 / certificate** — the engine hands over an object a checker can
  verify without redoing the search. `lp_potential` (exact rational pagoda),
  `ic3_pdr` (inductive invariant), `deadlock_carver` (dead-region theorem).
* **独立检查器 / independent checker** — a second implementation, written from
  the specification rather than the code, re-derives the answer. All eight
  rows have one; what varies is how much it shares with the subject, which is
  why every E11 partial carries a shared-dependency list.
* **对拍 / differential** — two implementations that were supposed to agree are
  run against each other. `fd_adapter`'s three rungs, the `recheck` package's
  same-certificate-two-rule-sets pairs, and `deadlock_carver`'s three
  independent encodings of `open4far` agreeing on 112 ground actions,
  3352 reachable states and an optimal plan of 11.

The distinction that matters is not which box a row is in but **what the
checker shares with the thing it checks**. E11's own lesson, learned by having
a result overturned: two oracles agreeing is one piece of evidence, not two,
when they share a premise.

## How to read the boundary column

Three things are kept apart, because collapsing them is how a table like this
lies:

* **Measured boundary** — somebody drew the line and reports where it is.
* **Pre-registered boundary** — the line was written down in `DECISIONS.md`
  before it was hit, and a later measurement quantified it. `zero_space`'s
  quantifier (D-003) and `lp_potential`'s incompleteness (D-014) are these; an
  adversarial review overturned the word "defect" on the first of them.
* **Self-consistent on the fitting evidence** — the re-check ran, and it ran
  on the same data the claim was fitted to. This is not a fourth kind of
  boundary but a qualifier that attaches to a row's *re-check* column, and
  until E17 no row in this rig had anything else. See the standing rule
  below.
* **边界未测** — nobody has drawn the line. `ic3_pdr` entire; the fuzz
  battery's view of a real Fast Downward rung; `lp_potential`'s infeasibility
  claim as a proof rather than a solver's word; `cegis_miner` past 3 literals
  and past the grid family; `probe_frontier`'s planner-backed path.

## The standing rule on the word 「已验证」

**Where no held-out validation exists, a cell may say 「在观测证据上自洽」 and
may not say 「已验证」.** Not as a stylistic preference — as the literal
reading of what was done. A re-check that consumes the evidence the claim was
fitted to answers "is this claim consistent with its own inputs", which for
`zero_space` is a tautology exactly: the laws *are* the null space of the
observed differences, so `verify()` re-checks `a·d = 0` on the same `d`.

**Every row's re-check column is qualified by this sentence, including the two
that now carry a held-out number.** E17 produced held-out validation for two
of the eight, and its own adversarial review then reduced what that buys:

* `zero_space`: **13.1 %** of global laws survive when one operation is withheld
  (7200 of 7200 withheld rows carry information the fit had not seen). The
  companion cut, a random 70 / 30 transition split, returns 100.0 % and is
  **vacuous**: 0 of its 2160 withheld rows are new. It is published as a
  control that failed, not as a result.
* `lp_potential`: **26.4 %** of certificates still satisfy `inv_closed` on a withheld
  move geometry, and 58 are outright false. Handed the same partial evidence the
  certificate was fitted on, the emit gate lets **1408** of them out, all
  58 false ones among them; it withholds all of them only when handed the
  complete graph, which a partial-evidence caller does not have.

Neither number is a score. `zero_space`'s quantifier is the observed evidence
by decision (D-003) and `lp_potential` is sound but incomplete (D-014), so a
miss measures how far a claim extrapolates and nothing else. What the two
numbers *do* settle is the wording: with them, those rows may state a
held-out figure with its cut named. Without them, no row may.

Corpus limits, since a rule with an unstated scope is worse than none: both
measurements are on synthetic families generated by the harness
(`parityworld`, `pegN`). No live-game data, and no other world family, has
been held out for any engine. The magnitudes are corpus-shaped — the review
showed both `zero_space` figures move by tens of points under an equally
defensible corpus geometry or split fraction.

Two numbers are corrections of figures that were circulating, and both run
against the rig's own interest:

* `lp_potential`'s incompleteness is **639 / 2189 = 29.2 %**, not the 46.6 %
  often quoted — that figure is the no-certificate rate, and 24.0 pp of it is the
  engine correctly refusing to prove something false.
* `deadlock_carver`'s pruning dividend is real against a blind search and **zero**
  against an admissible heuristic (47 -> 47 on `lmcut`). The frequency half of
  Theoria 1.9 stands; the speed-up half does not.

## What audits all eight, and what it does not reach

* The property battery runs 60 worlds per engine with 0 violations, and
  64 injected defects establish that every invariant has a working detection
  path — 14 of them survive, which is the honest half of that sentence.
* It covers **six** of the eight. `deadlock_carver` and `ic3_pdr` have no property
  module. `deadlock_carver` is nonetheless the most independently adjudicated
  process here — three separate routes, one of them Lean — and `ic3_pdr` is the
  least.
* Of the **111** leaf fields the six engines publish into `candidates.jsonl`,
  only **25** are asserted by an invariant. A further 22 are read but only as an
  index, a gate or inside an aggregate, and **64** are never audited at all. So the
  number of fields no invariant makes a claim about is **86**, not 64 — the
  distinction matters because these fields reach the manual, and from there the
  adjudicating LLM's beliefs, carrying no evidence at all.
* The certificate rechecker catalogues 31 ways to lie to it. **29** are refused;
  1 is accepted *with a recorded qualifier* (a dead region leaning on the
  declared constraint); and exactly **1** is accepted with no qualifier and is
  correct to accept — `delete-the-rule`, a certificate true of a rule set with
  a rule missing. That last one is Theoria §1.3 entire, no certificate checker
  can see it, and it is carried as `expect: NOT-CAUGHT` so the suite fails if
  it ever starts being caught.
