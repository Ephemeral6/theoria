## Rules

| name | when | then |
|---|---|---|
| `push` | act=D and the target cell is floor (or the opened Door cell) | Cart moves one cell in direction D |
| `blocked` | act=D and the target cell is a wall, the closed Door, or the Button | nothing moves |
| `press` | act=D and the target cell is the Button and the Button is unpressed | Button recolours 7 -> 8 AND the Door vanishes, in the same transition; the Cart does not move |
| `door_open` | (cascade of press, not an independent rule) | Door vanishes; the Door cell becomes passable forever after |
| `teleport` | act=D and the target cell is the Portal entry (7,3) | Cart moves to (1,1) |

## Invariants

* **cart_unique** — exactly one cell carries colour 6 at all times
* **button_latched** — once the Button is 8 it is never 7 again
* **right_room_locked (variant a0-no-button only)** — with no Button the Door never opens, so the Cart never occupies a cell of the right room; the parity of the Cart's occupancy over the right room is conserved at 0
