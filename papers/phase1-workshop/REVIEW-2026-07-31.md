## Ways the corrected text could still mislead, examined

1. **"196 of 299" read as "the fourth form now works."** The census bar is
   non-vacuity plus well-formedness; `crosscheck/FOUR_FORMS_TRUTH.md` §3 calls
   it a ceiling on correctness, not a floor on brokenness. First draft of the
   delta said "usable" for the post-repair count and was corrected during its
   own citecheck; §11.3 now carries the ceiling caveat explicitly, and the
   abstract repeats it. **Adequately fenced.**
2. **"Three of four forms verified" as the implied complement.** The exact
   trap `crosscheck/FOUR_FORMS_TRUTH.md` §6 warns against. The abstract, §2.1
   and §11.3 each state that the census measured the PDDL form only and that
   the other three are unmeasured by it. **Not asserted anywhere in the
   delta.** A reader wanting "three verified" is explicitly refused in §11.3.
3. **The provenance statement read as an accusation, or as retired by the
   repair.** "Every planning number ran through the world-specific generator"
   is about how the data was made; the repair does not rewrite history. §3.1
   and §11.3 both say the statement survives the repair and that no reported
   number was re-derived through the repaired backend. **Clear.** The converse
   risk — a reader concluding the reported plans are therefore suspect — is
   met by §11.3 pointing at the world-specific backend's own recorded defects
   (§5.8, §6.6) rather than pretending it is undocumented.
4. **The visibility-mechanism claim in §2.1 quietly restored to full
   strength.** The qualification says the mechanism was *inoperative* on the
   fourth form until 2026-07-31 and is now guarded in code
   (`theoria-arm/inner/books.py`, generator self-parse). What it does not and
   must not claim: that a guard proves the forms agree. The guard turns red on
   an undeclared failure; agreement between forms is still not certified by
   anything. The §2.1 text stops at "guarded", which is the defensible line.
5. **Figure 1's rendered label "four forms".** Unchanged, deliberately: the
   forms are co-derived post-repair, the figure depicts the A0 run whose four
   forms were all actually produced (planning via the world-specific backend,
   scoped two paragraphs above the figure caption in §3.1), and the SVGs are
   pinned by the figures pipeline. Recorded here as a residual rather than
   edited: a reader of the figure alone, without §3.1's scoping paragraph,
   still gets no hint of the two-backend history. Judged acceptable because
   the caption's own section now carries the scope.
6. **The abstract's exemption from the citation rule.** The delta adds five
   quantities to the abstract (0/303, 196/299, 103, 12, 2026-07-31). The
   exemption holds only if each recurs cited in the body: 0/303 and 196/299
   and 103 recur in §1.5 and §11.3 with `crosscheck/` citations; the 12-step
   figure recurs in §3.1 cited; the date recurs in §3.1 and §11.3 anchored to
   the run record and commit. **Exemption intact.**
7. **Does the delta dilute the limitations section?** §11.3 gained a long
   paragraph that ends in a repair story. The risk: a limitations section that
   narrates a fix reads as a victory lap. The paragraph keeps the pre-repair
   zero as the headline, states the frozen record stays frozen, and closes on
   the ceiling caveat rather than the fix. **Acceptable, borderline** — a
   future cut pass could shorten the repair half without losing the census.

## Verdict

The correction says what the evidence supports, refuses the two natural
overstatements ("the form works now", "three of four verified"), and leaves
the pre-repair record frozen. One residual is carried (item 5), one style
concern noted (item 7). No finding blocks.
