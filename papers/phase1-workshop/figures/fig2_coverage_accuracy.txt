Reversibility beats coverage. A0 saw nearly every reachable (state, action) pair and shipped a manual wrong in three places; A0-prime saw under half and shipped one with no errors. The variable is not how much was seen but whether what was seen could be seen again.

CAVEAT: Two runs, one variable changed, both worlds self-built and adjudicated by the same instance. A controlled comparison at n=1 per arm, not a statistical result.
