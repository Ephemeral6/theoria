-- note on reproducibility ------------------------------------------
The chain head differs between runs of this demo, and that is correct,
not drift: `prev` is the sha256 of the previous line's bytes, and those
bytes include that line's wall-clock `ts`. LEDGER_FORMAT.md says `ts` is
not part of any hash *or comparison*; the first half is loose -- `ts` is
inside the hashed bytes, one record later. That asymmetry is exactly what
broke ablation-arm's determinism check when the chain landed (fixed there
by naming `prev` a derived clock field and paying for it with a chain
verdict). What IS stable here is the verdict: 42 records, 3 arms, 0
duplicate seq, chain PASS.
